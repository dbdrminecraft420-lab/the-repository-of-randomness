# Committee of Unrelated Thoughts
## Second Department: Google Maps Chaos Department

**Established:** Whenever it felt right  
**Jurisdiction:** Reality as represented by Google Maps  
**Seriousness rating:** 2/10 (official)

### Mandate
The Google Maps Chaos Department is responsible for most of the random edits and new locations added to Google Maps that are:
- Wrong
- Slightly wrong
- Completely nonexistent
- Named after scooters, melons, or free time
- Positioned in the middle of a lake for no reason

### Known Activities
- Adding phantom parks that vanish when you arrive
- Mislabeling streets as "Scooter Priority Zone"
- Placing the MelonKing’s summer palace at coordinates that do not exist
- Creating bike paths that loop into themselves
- Renaming perfectly normal buildings to "Committee of Unrelated Thoughts — Annex B"

### Official Statement
"If a place appears on the map and then disappoints you in person, that was probably us. You’re welcome."

— Google Maps Chaos Department  
*A proud subsidiary of the Committee of Unrelated Thoughts*
