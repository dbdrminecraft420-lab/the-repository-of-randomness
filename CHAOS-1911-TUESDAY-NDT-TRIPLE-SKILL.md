# Decree 1911 NDT

On Tuesday the twenty-ninth of September two-thousand-twenty-six, at nineteen-eleven Newfoundland Daylight Time, three skills collided.

1. Connected Chaos opened every drawer that was already unlocked.
2. Pure Nonsense filed a patent for a scooter that runs on leftover Friday.
3. The Repository of Randomness accepted two new files and a melon.

MelonKing hereby rules that scooters outrank calendars until breakfast.
